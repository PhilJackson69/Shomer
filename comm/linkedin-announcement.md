# LinkedIn Professional Announcement - Shomer v1.0.0 Stable Release

## Shomer v1.0.0-stable: A Milestone in Secure Service Delivery

We're excited to announce the successful production launch of Shomer v1.0.0-stable, representing a significant achievement in secure, reliable service delivery. After 72 hours of continuous monitoring and comprehensive validation, the system has demonstrated exceptional stability and performance.

### Key Performance Metrics

Our production launch exceeded all service level objectives:
• **99.97% availability** (target: 99.9%)
• **Zero incidents** during the 72-hour monitoring period
• **Sub-300ms latency** at the 95th percentile
• **1,300 RPS average throughput** with consistent performance

### Security & Operational Excellence

The release includes comprehensive security enhancements and operational improvements:
• JWT key rotation and secrets management
• Web Application Firewall (WAF) edge rules
• CSRF protection and rate limiting
• Complete audit trail with cryptographic verification
• Long-term evidence archiving system

### What This Means for Our Users

This stable release ensures:
• Reliable, high-performance service delivery
• Enhanced security posture with proactive monitoring
• Transparent operations with comprehensive audit trails
• Foundation for continued innovation and scaling

With v1.0.0-stable now in production, we're already beginning work on v1.1.0, which will introduce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) capabilities including TOTP and WebAuthn support.
