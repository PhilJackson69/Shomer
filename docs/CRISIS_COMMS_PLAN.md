## Purpose

This plan outlines procedures for responding to crises that threaten Shomer ICE Alerts' reputation, user trust, or community safety. Quick, transparent, and empathetic communication is essential to maintaining the community's trust.

---

## Crisis Definitions

### Level 1: Critical (Immediate Response Required)

- **Privacy Breach:** User data exposed or compromised
- **False Alert - Active:** Incorrect "active" severity causing immediate panic
- **Partner Breach:** Partner organization compromises system or shares data improperly
- **System Compromise:** Hacking, unauthorized access, or security vulnerability exploited
- **Legal Action:** Lawsuit, government seizure, or court order

**Response Time:** Within 1 hour  
**Leadership:** CEO + Legal + Oversight Board

### Level 2: High (Urgent Response Required)

- **False Alert - Verified:** Incorrect "verified" alert published
- **Moderation Failure:** Multiple alerts with PII or inappropriate content published
- **Partner Misconduct:** Partner violates policies (but no data breach)
- **Media Crisis:** Negative national media coverage
- **Community Backlash:** Significant community criticism or loss of trust

**Response Time:** Within 4 hours  
**Leadership:** Communications Director + Community Lead

### Level 3: Moderate (Standard Response)

- **False Alert - Rumor:** Incorrect "rumor" level alert
- **Technical Outage:** Service unavailable for >2 hours
- **Minor Privacy Concern:** Process issue, no actual breach
- **Staff Error:** Internal mistake without external impact
- **Moderate Media Attention:** Local news inquiry

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours  
**Leadership:** Operations Manager

## Crisis Scenarios & Response Playbooks

### Scenario 1: False Alert - Active Level

**Trigger:** Alert marked "active" published but was incorrect/outdated

**Impact:** High - Community panic, loss of trust, potential safety concerns

#### Immediate Actions (Within 15 Minutes)

1. **Verify:** Confirm alert is false
   - Contact original source
   - Check with partner organizations
   - Review submission evidence

2. **Retract:** Send correction to all recipients
   ```
   🚨 ALERT CORRECTION

   Our [time] alert about [location] was incorrect. 
   [Specific correction - e.g., "Activity was not ongoing" or "Location was misreported"]

   We sincerely apologize for this error. We are investigating and improving our verification process.

   Questions: support@shomer.app
   ```

3. **Notify Leadership:** Alert Crisis Lead and Oversight Board

4. **Document:** Preserve all evidence, logs, and communications

#### Short-Term Actions (Within 4 Hours)

5. **Investigate:** 
   - How did this happen?
   - Who reviewed the alert?
   - What checks failed?

6. **Public Statement:**
   ```
   Statement on [Date] Alert

   On [date/time], we published an incorrect ICE activity alert for [location]. 
   The alert indicated "active" status, but [correct information].

   We immediately:
   - Sent a correction to all recipients
   - Launched an internal investigation
   - Suspended similar alerts pending review

   We sincerely apologize. Our community's trust is our highest priority. 
   We are implementing additional verification steps to prevent this from happening again.

   We will share our findings and improvements within 48 hours.

   [Name, Title]
   ```

7. **Community Outreach:**
   - Email to all subscribers
   - Post on website
   - Social media statement
   - Partner organization notification

#### Long-Term Actions (Within 7 Days)

8. **Root Cause Analysis:**
   - Full investigation report
   - Identification of failure points
   - Systemic issues

9. **Process Improvements:**
   - Updated moderation guidelines
   - Additional verification steps
   - Staff retraining
   - Technology enhancements

10. **Transparency Report:**
    - Public report on incident
    - Steps taken to prevent recurrence
    - Accountability measures
    - Commitment to improvement

11. **Community Feedback:**
    - Open forum for questions
    - Feedback collection
    - Address concerns individually

### Scenario 2: Partner Organization Breach

**Trigger:** Partner organization compromises system security, shares data improperly, or violates policies

**Impact:** Critical - Privacy violation, legal liability, trust damage

#### Immediate Actions (Within 1 Hour)

1. **Suspend Access:**
   - Immediately revoke partner's system access
   - Lock down their account
   - Preserve audit logs

2. **Assess Damage:**
   - What data was accessed?
   - What was shared/compromised?
   - How many users affected?

3. **Secure Systems:**
   - Change relevant credentials
   - Verify no other access points compromised
   - Security audit of all partner accounts

4. **Legal Notification:**
   - Notify legal counsel immediately
   - Determine legal obligations (breach notification laws)
   - Assess partnership agreement violations

#### Short-Term Actions (Within 4 Hours)

5. **User Notification:** (If data compromised)
   ```
   IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE

   We are writing to inform you of a security incident involving one of our partner organizations.

   What happened:
   [Brief, factual description]

   What data was affected:
   [Specific data types - be transparent]

   What we're doing:
   - Immediately suspended the partner's access
   - Launched full investigation
   - Enhanced security measures
   - Notified appropriate authorities

   What you should do:
   [Specific actions for users]

   We take this very seriously. Your trust is paramount.

   Questions: security@shomer.app
   Dedicated hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX
   ```

6. **Oversight Board Emergency Meeting:**
   - Within 2 hours of discovery
   - Full briefing
   - Determine next steps

7. **Partner Contact:**
   - Formal written notification
   - Meeting with partner leadership
   - Determine if breach was intentional or error

#### Long-Term Actions (Within 14 Days)

8. **Investigation:**
   - Third-party forensic investigation
   - Full scope of breach
   - Legal review

9. **Partnership Decision:**
   - Termination if intentional breach
   - Probation and retraining if error
   - Enhanced monitoring if reinstated

10. **Policy Updates:**
    - Revised partnership agreements
    - Enhanced security requirements
    - Additional audit procedures

11. **Community Accountability:**
    - Public report (redacted for legal reasons)
    - Process improvements
    - Commitment to enhanced oversight

### Scenario 3: Privacy Breach / Data Exposure

**Trigger:** User data exposed due to hack, error, or system vulnerability

**Impact:** Critical - Legal liability, user harm, severe trust damage

#### Immediate Actions (Within 1 Hour)

1. **Contain:**
   - Isolate affected systems
   - Stop data flow
   - Preserve evidence

2. **Assess:**
   - Number of users affected
   - Type of data exposed
   - Duration of exposure
   - Who accessed the data

3. **Legal Obligations:**
   - 72-hour GDPR notification requirement
   - State-specific breach notification laws
   - Notify legal counsel immediately

4. **Oversight Board:**
   - Emergency notification
   - Legal authority consultation

#### Short-Term Actions (Within 24 Hours)

5. **User Notification:**
   ```
   URGENT: Security Breach Notification

   We are writing to inform you that your personal information may have been exposed in a security incident.

   What happened:
   [Clear, honest description]

   What information was involved:
   [Specific data types]

   How many people were affected:
   [Number or "all users"]

   What we are doing:
   - Secured the vulnerability immediately
   - Launched forensic investigation
   - Notified law enforcement [if applicable]
   - Retained cybersecurity experts
   - Enhanced security measures

   What we recommend you do:
   [Specific steps - password changes, monitoring, etc.]

   What we are offering:
   - [Free credit monitoring if financial data involved]
   - Dedicated support hotline
   - Regular updates

   We take full responsibility. This is unacceptable, and we are committed to doing better.

   Detailed FAQ: shomer.app/breach-faq
   Questions: security@shomer.app
   Hotline: 1-800-XXX-XXXX (24/7)

   [CEO/Executive Director Name]
   ```

6. **Public Statement:**
   - Website banner
   - Social media
   - Press release
   - Partner notification

7. **Regulatory Notification:**
   - Data protection authorities (GDPR)
   - State attorneys general
   - FTC (if applicable)

#### Long-Term Actions (Within 30 Days)

8. **Third-Party Investigation:**
   - Independent forensic analysis
   - Comprehensive security audit
   - Vulnerability assessment

9. **Remediation:**
   - Fix all identified vulnerabilities
   - Implement enhanced security
   - Staff training
   - Process improvements

10. **Accountability:**
    - Public report (when investigation complete)
    - Leadership accountability
    - Policy changes
    - Ongoing monitoring

11. **Rebuild Trust:**
    - Transparency about improvements
    - Regular security updates
    - Community input on policies
    - Enhanced oversight

### Scenario 4: Widespread Media Crisis

**Trigger:** Negative national media coverage, viral social media criticism, or public figure condemnation

**Impact:** High - Reputation damage, user churn, partner concerns

#### Immediate Actions (Within 2 Hours)

1. **Monitor:**
   - Set up media monitoring
   - Track social media mentions
   - Identify key narratives

2. **Assess:**
   - Is criticism valid?
   - What is the core issue?
   - Who are the critics?
   - What is the ask/demand?

3. **Holding Statement:**
   ```
   We are aware of [concerns/criticism] regarding [issue]. 
   We take these concerns seriously and are reviewing the situation. 
   We will provide a full response within [timeframe].
   ```

#### Short-Term Actions (Within 24 Hours)

4. **Response Strategy:**
   - If criticism is valid: Acknowledge, apologize, commit to improvement
   - If criticism is based on misunderstanding: Clarify, educate, provide facts
   - If criticism is malicious: Defend with facts, but don't engage in flame war

5. **Community First:**
   - Prioritize existing community over critics
   - Direct communication with users
   - Partner organization outreach

6. **Media Response:**
   - Prepare spokesperson
   - Key messages document
   - Q&A prep
   - Interviews (if appropriate)

7. **Statement:**
   ```
   [Acknowledge issue]
   [Our values/mission]
   [Factual response]
   [What we're doing]
   [Commitment to community]
   ```

#### Long-Term Actions (Within 14 Days)

8. **Transparency:**
   - Blog post or public update
   - Data and evidence
   - Process explanations
   - Commitment to improvement

9. **Community Engagement:**
   - Town hall or Q&A
   - Feedback incorporation
   - Policy updates if needed

10. **Monitor & Adjust:**
    - Continuing monitoring
    - Respond to ongoing concerns
    - Build positive narrative

## Communication Principles

### Golden Rules

1. **Speed + Accuracy:** Fast, but never at the expense of accuracy
2. **Transparency:** Default to disclosure (within legal constraints)
3. **Empathy:** Acknowledge impact on people, not just systems
4. **Accountability:** Take responsibility, don't deflect or make excuses
5. **Action-Oriented:** Always include what we're doing about it
6. **Community-Centered:** Prioritize users over PR

### What to Say

✅ "We made a mistake, and we're sorry"  
✅ "Here's exactly what happened"  
✅ "This is what we're doing to fix it"  
✅ "Here's how we'll prevent this in the future"  
✅ "We're committed to earning back your trust"

### What NOT to Say

❌ "No comment"  
❌ "It wasn't that bad"  
❌ "Other services do this too"  
❌ "It's the user's fault"  
❌ "You're overreacting"  
❌ Technical jargon without explanation

### Bilingual Communication

**All crisis communications must be simultaneously released in English and Español:**
- Statements
- FAQs
- User notifications
- Media responses

**Process:**
1. Draft in both languages (not translation, co-creation)
2. Native speaker review
3. Cultural sensitivity check
4. Simultaneous release

## Post-Crisis Actions

### After Action Review (Within 7 Days)

1. **Timeline Reconstruction:**
   - What happened when
   - Who knew what when
   - Decision points

2. **Response Evaluation:**
   - What worked
   - What didn't
   - Gaps in process

3. **Lessons Learned:**
   - What would we do differently
   - What new procedures needed

4. **Plan Updates:**
   - Update this crisis plan
   - Train team on updates

### Trust Rebuilding (30-90 Days)

1. **Transparency Report:**
   - Publish detailed incident report
   - Include improvements made
   - Commit to ongoing updates

2. **Community Engagement:**
   - Town halls or listening sessions
   - Incorporate feedback
   - Visible changes

3. **Policy Updates:**
   - Strengthen relevant policies
   - Public comment period
   - Implementation

4. **Ongoing Communication:**
   - Regular updates on improvements
   - Metrics on progress
   - Sustained engagement
