# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 14, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 14, 2025

## Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Shomer, protecting your privacy is fundamental to our mission of keeping communities safe. We believe that effective security doesn't require sacrificing privacy. This policy explains, in plain English, how we collect, use, and protect your information.

**Key Principles:**
- 🔒 **Data Minimization**: We collect only what we need
- ⏰ **Limited Retention**: We delete data on a regular schedule
- 🚫 **No Facial Recognition**: We never use facial recognition technology
- ✋ **Your Control**: You decide what to share
- 📧 **Open Communication**: Questions welcome at privacy@shomer.local

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 When You Create an Account

If you're a registered user (staff, moderators, administrators), we collect:
- **Email address** (for login and notifications)
- **Password** (encrypted - we never see your actual password)
- **Name** (optional - you can use a pseudonym)
- **Role and permissions** (to control what you can access)

### 1.2 When You Submit a Tip

Our tip submission is designed for **anonymous reporting**. You choose what to share:

**Required:**
- Incident description
- Location (general area is fine)
- Date/time

**Optional (you decide):**
- Contact information (email or phone)
- Photos or videos
- Your name

**What we automatically record:**
- Submission timestamp
- IP address (for spam prevention only, deleted after 7 days)

### 1.3 Photos and Videos

When you upload media:
- **EXIF data is automatically removed** (GPS coordinates, camera make/model, timestamps)
- **File hash is generated** (to detect duplicates and ensure integrity)
- **Original can be preserved** (only if legally required and you consent)

**We never:**
- Use facial recognition technology
- Share photos publicly
- Sell or monetize images

### 1.4 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we collect:
- **Technical data**: Browser type, device type, operating system
- **Usage data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Error logs**: To fix bugs and improve the platform

**We do NOT:**
- Track you across other websites
- Use advertising cookies
- Build consumer profiles for marketing

### 2.1 Primary Purposes

We use your information to:

3. **Communicate**: Send you security updates, respond to your questions
4. **Improve the system**: Analyze trends (aggregated data only), fix issues

### 2.2 What We DON'T Do

We will **NEVER**:
- ❌ Sell your personal information
- ❌ Share your data with advertisers
- ❌ Use your information for unrelated purposes
- ❌ Track you for commercial gain
- ❌ Share with third parties (except as required by law)

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

### 3.1 Automatic Deletion Schedule

We believe in data minimization. Here's when we automatically delete information:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Exceptions |
|-----------|------------------|------------|
| **Anonymous tips** | 14 days | Tips marked "escalated" or "under investigation" |
| **Incident reports** | 1 year | Ongoing investigations, legal holds |
| **User accounts** | While active + 90 days after deactivation | None |
| **Audit logs** | 365 days | Legal/compliance requirements may extend |
| **IP addresses** | 7 days | Deleted automatically |
| **Photos (cleaned)** | 30 days | Legally required evidence retained longer |
| **Session data** | 24 hours | None |

### 3.2 Exceptions to Automatic Deletion

Data may be retained longer if:
- **Legal obligation**: Law enforcement request, court order, ongoing investigation
- **Safety necessity**: Active threat, unresolved incident
- **Your consent**: You explicitly request we keep your information

### 3.3 Your Right to Delete

**You can request deletion anytime** by emailing privacy@shomer.local

We will delete your information within **30 days** unless:
- We're legally required to keep it
- It's necessary for an active investigation
- It's already been automatically deleted

## 4. Photo and Video Privacy

### 4.1 EXIF Data Removal

Every photo and video uploaded is automatically processed to remove:
- **GPS coordinates** (where the photo was taken)
- **Camera information** (make, model, serial number)
- **Timestamps** (when the photo was taken)
- **Device identifiers** (phone ID, etc.)

This happens **automatically** - you don't need to do anything.

### 4.2 Original Preservation

**Default:** We store only the cleaned version (EXIF removed)

**Legal hold option:** If you're submitting evidence for legal proceedings, you can request we preserve the original. This requires:
- Explicit consent checkbox
- Clear legal justification
- Limited to authorized personnel only

### 4.3 Facial Recognition Ban

**We do NOT and will NOT:**
- Use facial recognition on submitted photos
- Identify individuals in images automatically
- Share images with facial recognition databases
- Use AI to analyze faces

### 4.4 Guidelines for Submitters

**Before uploading photos:**
- ❌ **Don't include faces** (unless they've consented or it's legally justified)
- ❌ **Don't photograph minors** (unless necessary for safety)
- ✅ **Do blur or crop** identifying features when possible
- ✅ **Do focus on** the incident, not people

### 5.3 Your Consent Required

We ask for your **explicit consent** before:

## 6. Security Measures

### 6.1 How We Protect Your Data

**Technical safeguards:**
- 🔐 **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- 🔑 **Password security**: Bcrypt hashing with salt (we never see your password)
- 🚪 **Access controls**: Role-based permissions, 2-factor authentication available
- 📊 **Audit logging**: Every access to sensitive data is logged
- 🔄 **Regular backups**: Encrypted and securely stored

**Operational safeguards:**
- Background checks for staff with data access
- Mandatory privacy training
- Incident response plan
- Regular security audits
- Penetration testing

### 6.2 What You Can Do

**Protect your account:**
- ✅ Use a strong, unique password
- ✅ Enable two-factor authentication
- ✅ Don't share your login credentials
- ✅ Log out on shared devices
- ✅ Report suspicious activity immediately
