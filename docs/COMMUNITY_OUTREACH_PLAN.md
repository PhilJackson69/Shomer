# Community Outreach Plan - ICE Alert System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Launch Date:** Target Q4 2025  
**Owner:** Community Engagement Team  
**Status:** Pre-Launch Planning

---

## Executive Summary

This outreach plan guides the community-centered launch of Shomer's ICE Alert system. The plan prioritizes trust-building, cultural competence, and sustainable partnerships with immigrant-serving organizations. Success is measured not by rapid growth, but by deep community trust and effective service delivery.

---

## Core Values

1. **Community-Led** - Local organizations and community members guide implementation
2. **Bilingual by Default** - All materials in English and Español from day one
3. **Trust First** - Relationship-building precedes technology rollout
4. **Trauma-Informed** - Recognition of community stress and fear
5. **Sustainable** - Long-term partnerships, not one-time campaigns

---

## Target Communities

### Primary Audiences

1. **Mixed-Status Families**
   - Households with varying immigration statuses
   - Parents of US-born children
   - Heightened vulnerability and information needs

2. **Recent Arrivals**
   - Immigrants within first 3 years in US
   - Limited familiarity with local resources
   - High need for rights education

3. **Sanctuary-Seeking Individuals**
   - Active cases or pending hearings
   - High anxiety about enforcement
   - Connected to legal aid networks

4. **Community Allies**
   - Teachers, healthcare workers, social workers
   - Want to support immigrant neighbors
   - Distribution nodes for information

### Geographic Focus

**Phase 1 (Pilot):**
- 3 urban neighborhoods with high immigrant populations
- Partner with established community organizations
- Focus on trust-building over scale

**Phase 2 (Expansion):**
- Additional neighborhoods based on pilot learnings
- Suburban and rural communities
- Cross-community collaboration

## Bilingual Content Strategy

### Language Principles

1. **Simultaneous Development** - English and Español created together, not translation
2. **Cultural Adaptation** - Not word-for-word, but culturally appropriate
3. **Plain Language** - Accessible to varying literacy levels
4. **Community Review** - Native speakers review all materials

### Content Types

| Content | Languages | Format | Owner |
|---------|-----------|--------|-------|
| Alert messages | EN / ES | SMS, email, push | Content team |
| Know Your Rights guide | EN / ES | Web, PDF | Legal team |
| Marketing materials | EN / ES | Print, digital | Marketing |
| Training materials | EN / ES | Slides, handouts | Training team |
| Policy documents | EN | Web, PDF | Policy team |
| FAQs | EN / ES | Web | Support team |

### Translation Process

1. **Content Creation:**
   - English and Español drafts created in parallel
   - Cultural consultants review both
   - Plain language review

2. **Community Review:**
   - Test with community members
   - Gather feedback on clarity
   - Iterate based on input

3. **Professional Review:**
   - Professional translator review
   - Legal terminology verification
   - Final approval

### Bilingual UI/UX

**App/Web Features:**
- Language toggle prominently displayed
- User preference saved
- Consistent terminology
- Right-to-left support (if expanding to Arabic)

**Messaging:**
- SMS alerts in user's preferred language
- Email subject lines bilingual
- Push notifications in preferred language

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Privacy breach | Low | Critical | Strong policies, training, audits |
| Misinformation spread | Medium | High | Human moderation, verification |
| Community distrust | Medium | High | Transparency, partnership model |
| Vigilante response | Low | High | Clear guidelines, education |
| Government surveillance | Medium | High | Legal protections, minimal data |
| Partner conflict | Low | Medium | Clear agreements, mediation |
| System abuse | Medium | Medium | Moderation, rate limiting |

### Crisis Communication Plan

**If Privacy Breach:**
1. Immediate notification to affected individuals
2. Public disclosure within 24 hours
3. Third-party audit
4. System pause if necessary
5. Corrective action plan

**If Misinformation:**
1. Immediate correction sent
2. Alert retraction if needed
3. Moderator retraining
4. Policy review

**If Community Backlash:**
1. Listen and acknowledge concerns
2. Community meeting within 48 hours
3. Partnership review
4. Adjust approach based on feedback
