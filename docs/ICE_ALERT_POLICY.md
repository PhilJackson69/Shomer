## Overview

Shomer's ICE Alert system provides community-based notifications about reported immigration enforcement activity. This policy governs the collection, verification, and distribution of these alerts to ensure accuracy, protect privacy, and uphold dignity while serving community safety needs.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Human Dignity First** - All alerts prioritize the safety and dignity of community members
2. **Accuracy Over Speed** - Every alert undergoes human review before publication
3. **Privacy Protection** - No personal information is collected, stored, or distributed
4. **Opt-In Only** - Subscribers must explicitly consent to receive ICE alerts
5. **Transparency** - All moderation decisions and processes are documented

---

## Scope

### What We Alert On

ICE alerts cover **public or partner-verified information only**:

✅ **Allowed Content:**
- General area/neighborhood descriptions (e.g., "Mission District", "Downtown")
- Type of activity observed (e.g., "ICE vehicles observed", "Checkpoint reported")
- Time and date of observation
- General vicinity radius (not specific addresses)
- Publicly observable activity

❌ **Prohibited Content:**
- Specific home or business addresses
- Names of individuals (targets, witnesses, or officers)
- License plate numbers or vehicle identifiers beyond general description
- Badge numbers or officer identifications
- Photos or videos showing faces of any individuals
- Social security numbers or any PII
- Speculation about deportation targets or investigations
- Unverified rumors without clear disclaimer

## Data Handling

### Information We Collect

**For Each Alert:**
- General location (area/neighborhood)
- Description of reported activity
- Source type (community member, verified partner)
- Date and time submitted
- Severity level (rumor, verified, active)

**We DO NOT Collect:**
- Submitter identity (if anonymous)
- Personal information about any individuals
- Specific addresses or locations that could identify residences
- Images or video footage

### Information We Distribute

**Public Alert Content:**
- Sanitized location description
- Alert description (after moderation)
- Severity level
- Timestamp
- General vicinity (approximate coordinates, if provided)

**NOT Included in Public Alerts:**
- Submitter information
- Internal moderation notes
- Confidence scores (internal only)
- Rejected content reasons

### Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Archive Location |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------|
| Active alerts | 48 hours | Public feed |
| Archived alerts | 7 days | Secure database |
| Moderation logs | 90 days | Admin only |
| Broadcast logs | 30 days | Admin audit |
| Statistical aggregates | Indefinite | Analytics |

**After 48 Hours:**
- Alerts automatically expire from public feed
- Content moves to secure archive
- Only accessible by moderators for review
- Not searchable by public

**After 7 Days:**
- Individual alert details purged
- Only aggregate statistics retained
- No personally identifiable patterns kept

## Verification Process

### Submission Requirements

1. **Who Can Submit:**
   - Verified community partners
   - Moderators and administrators
   - Vetted legal aid organizations
   - Sanctuary congregation liaisons

2. **Verification Workflow:**

```
Submission → Automated PII Check → Human Review → Confidence Scoring → Approval/Rejection → Publication
```

**Automated Checks:**
- Pattern matching for PII (SSN, license plates, etc.)
- Prohibited term detection
- Content length validation
- Geographic bounds verification

**Human Moderation:**
- Every alert reviewed by trained moderator
- Compliance with content policy verified
- Context and credibility assessed
- Edits for clarity and safety if needed

**Confidence Scoring:**
- Source reliability (0-0.3 weight)
- Content specificity (0-0.3 weight)
- Corroboration (0-0.4 weight)
- **Threshold:** 0.5 minimum for publication

### Severity Levels

| Level | Definition | Criteria | Expiration |
|-------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Rumor** | Unconfirmed community report | Single source, no verification | 24 hours |
| **Verified** | Confirmed by trusted source | Multiple sources or verified partner | 48 hours |
| **Active** | Current, ongoing activity | Real-time report from verified source | 48 hours |

**Escalation:**
- Rumors can be upgraded to Verified with additional confirmation
- Verified can be upgraded to Active if ongoing
- Active downgraded to Verified after activity concludes

## Subscriber Consent

### Opt-In Requirements

**ICE alerts are OPT-IN ONLY.** Users must:

1. **Explicit Consent:**
   - Check "I want to receive ICE activity alerts"
   - Separate from general alert preferences
   - Bilingual consent language (English/Español)

2. **Understanding:**
   - Review "What to expect" documentation
   - Acknowledge alerts are community-reported
   - Confirm understanding of alert purpose

3. **Preferences:**
   - Select delivery channels (SMS, email, push)
   - Set geographic radius
   - Choose severity levels to receive

**Consent Language:**
> "I understand that ICE alerts are community-submitted reports that have been human-reviewed but may not be 100% accurate. I opt in to receive these alerts to stay informed about reported activity in my area. I can opt out at any time."

### Opt-Out Process

Users can opt out at ANY time through:
- Account settings toggle
- Text reply "STOP ICE ALERTS" to SMS number
- Unsubscribe link in emails
- Support contact: community@shomer.app

**Immediate Effect:**
- Opt-out processed within 5 minutes
- No further ICE alerts sent
- Other Shomer services unaffected
- Re-opt-in available anytime

## Moderation Standards

### Moderator Training

**Required Training (8 hours):**
1. Privacy and PII protection (2 hours)
2. Immigration law basics (2 hours)
3. Community trauma-informed practices (2 hours)
4. Shomer platform and policies (2 hours)

**Ongoing:**
- Monthly review sessions
- Quarterly policy updates
- Case study discussions

### Review Process

**Every Alert Reviewed for:**

1. **Safety:**
   - Does NOT endanger any individuals
   - Does NOT reveal protected information
   - Does NOT target specific people

2. **Accuracy:**
   - Information is verifiable or properly disclaimed
   - Source is credible
   - Location is appropriately vague

3. **Compliance:**
   - Follows all policy guidelines
   - No prohibited content included
   - Appropriate severity level

**Decision Timeline:**
- Standard review: Within 15 minutes
- Complex cases: Within 1 hour
- Escalations: Within 2 hours

### Rejection Criteria

Alerts are REJECTED if they contain:

- Personal identifying information (names, addresses, etc.)
- Unverified speculation presented as fact
- Content that could enable retaliation
- Prohibited imagery or descriptions
- Inflammatory or biased language
- Incomplete or unclear information

**Rejection Process:**
1. Moderator documents reason
2. Submitter notified (if identifiable)
3. Guidance provided for resubmission
4. Rejection logged for transparency report

## Content Moderation

### Prohibited Content

**Absolutely Prohibited:**
- Names of any individuals (targets, witnesses, officers, agents)
- Specific addresses or locations that could identify homes/businesses
- License plates, vehicle VINs, badge numbers
- Photos/videos showing faces
- Social Security Numbers or government IDs
- Phone numbers, email addresses
- Speculation about specific deportation targets
- Content encouraging vigilante action or retaliation

**Discouraged (Requires Justification):**
- Very specific timing (use approximate windows)
- Vehicle descriptions beyond general type
- Clothing descriptions that could identify individuals
- Information obtained through questionable means

### Editing Guidelines

**Moderators May Edit to:**
- Remove PII while preserving alert meaning
- Broaden overly specific locations
- Clarify unclear descriptions
- Correct factual errors
- Improve readability (grammar, spelling)

**Moderators May NOT:**
- Change the fundamental meaning of alert
- Add information not in original submission
- Editorialize or inject opinion
- Remove content to suppress legitimate alerts

**Major Edits:**
- Require submitter approval if identifiable
- Documented in moderation log
- Reviewed by senior moderator

## Privacy & Security

### Technical Safeguards

1. **Data Minimization:**
   - Collect only essential information
   - Purge data according to retention schedule
   - No IP logging for public submissions

2. **Encryption:**
   - TLS for all data in transit
   - At-rest encryption for database
   - Encrypted backups

3. **Access Control:**
   - Role-based access (RBAC)
   - Audit logs for all admin actions
   - Multi-factor authentication required

4. **Anonymization:**
   - Optional anonymous submission
   - IP addresses not linked to alerts
   - Location data approximated for privacy

### Legal Compliance

**Compliance Standards:**
- GDPR (for international users)
- CCPA (California users)
- State-specific privacy laws
- Federal communication regulations

**Law Enforcement Requests:**
- Require valid subpoena
- Reviewed by legal counsel
- Community notification if legally permitted
- Minimal disclosure principle
