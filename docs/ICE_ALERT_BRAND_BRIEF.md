## Mission & Values

### Feature Mission

Shomer ICE Alerts provides verified, community-based notifications about reported immigration enforcement activity. We empower communities to make informed decisions about their safety while protecting privacy and upholding dignity.

### Brand Values

1. **Accuracy Over Speed**
   - Human-reviewed before publication
   - Verified sources prioritized
   - Clear distinction between rumor and confirmed

2. **Calm, Not Panic**
   - Measured, factual tone
   - Resources and rights information included
   - De-escalation through information

3. **Solidarity**
   - Community-driven and community-serving
   - Partnership-based model
   - "We're all in this together" spirit

4. **Dignity & Privacy First**
   - No personal information ever
   - Respectful language
   - Trauma-informed communication

5. **Transparency & Trust**
   - Clear policies and procedures
   - Public accountability metrics
   - Honest about limitations

## Visual Identity

### Logo & Iconography

**Primary Icon:**
- 🔶 Orange shield with beacon/light symbol
- Represents: Protection + Awareness

**Alternative Icon:**
- Hand holding light/candle
- Represents: Community care and guidance

**Do NOT use:**
- Sirens or emergency symbols (too alarming)
- Surveillance imagery (contradicts privacy values)
- Aggressive or combative symbols

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage | Meaning |
|-------|-----|-------|---------|
| **Shomer Blue** | #1E40AF | Primary brand | Trust, stability |
| **Alert Orange** | #EA580C | ICE alerts accent | Warning (not panic) |
| **Success Green** | #16A34A | Verified status | Safety, confirmation |
| **Neutral Gray** | #6B7280 | Rumor status | Unconfirmed, cautious |
| **Alert Red** | #DC2626 | Active/critical | Immediate attention |
| **Warm White** | #FFFBEB | Backgrounds | Calm, approachable |

**Color Usage Guidelines:**
- **Orange** is the primary ICE alert color
- Use sparingly for emphasis, not overwhelming
- Balance orange with calming blues and neutrals
- Red reserved for truly critical/active situations only

### Typography

**Primary Font:** Inter  
- Clean, modern, highly legible
- Excellent multilingual support
- Good at small sizes (mobile)

**Headings:** Inter Bold (600-700 weight)  
**Body:** Inter Regular (400 weight)  
**Emphasis:** Inter Medium (500 weight)  
**Captions:** Inter Regular (400 weight, 14px)

**Bilingual Considerations:**
- Slightly larger font sizes for Spanish (diacritics)
- Test with longer Spanish translations
- Ensure readability at 16px minimum

### Design Cues

**Aesthetic:**
- Clean, minimalist
- Approachable, not cold
- Professional but warm
- Mobile-first

**Visual Style:**
- Rounded corners (8px radius)
- Subtle shadows for elevation
- Clear hierarchy
- Generous white space

**Avoid:**
- Overly technical/corporate feel
- Childish or patronizing design
- Cluttered layouts
- Aggressive red/black color schemes

## Tone & Voice

### Voice Characteristics

| Characteristic | We Are | We Are NOT |
|----------------|--------|------------|
| **Tone** | Calm, informative | Alarming, sensational |
| **Authority** | Knowledgeable guide | Authoritarian command |
| **Empathy** | Understanding, supportive | Pitying, patronizing |
| **Clarity** | Clear, direct | Vague, bureaucratic |
| **Respect** | Dignified, respectful | Casual, flippant |

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- ✅ Use active voice
- ✅ Be specific and concrete
- ✅ Lead with most important information
- ✅ Include actionable next steps
- ✅ Acknowledge emotions ("This can be stressful...")
- ✅ Use "we" and "community" (collective)

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use all caps (except acronyms)
- ❌ Multiple exclamation points
- ❌ Vague warnings ("be careful")
- ❌ Legal jargon without explanation
- ❌ Assumptions about reader's situation
- ❌ "Us vs. them" language

### Sample Messaging

**Alert Message (Good):**
> ⚠️ ICE Activity Reported - Verified
> 
> Area: Mission District  
> Time: Today, 2:00 PM  
> Description: ICE vehicles observed near BART station. Activity ongoing.
> 
> Know your rights: Do not open the door without a warrant. You have the right to remain silent.
> 
> Resources: shomer.app/ice-guide  
> Legal help: 1-800-555-0100

**Alert Message (Bad):**
> 🚨🚨🚨 ICE RAID HAPPENING NOW!!!
> 
> Mission District - RUN! HIDE! ICE EVERYWHERE!
> 
> SHARE THIS WITH EVERYONE YOU KNOW!

## Bilingual Strategy

### Principles

1. **Simultaneous Creation**
   - English and Español developed together
   - Not translation, but cultural adaptation

2. **Cultural Respect**
   - Idioms and phrases appropriate to each language
   - Localized references when relevant
   - Reviewed by native speakers

3. **Consistency**
   - Terminology consistent across both languages
   - Tone matches in both languages
   - Visual balance (text length)

### Key Terms

| English | Español | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| ICE Alert | Alerta de ICE | Use acronym in both |
| Know Your Rights | Conozca Sus Derechos | Standard phrasing |
| Stay informed | Manténgase informado/a | Gender-inclusive when possible |
| Verified | Verificado | |
| Community | Comunidad | Central to both |

### Language Toggle

- Prominent placement (top right)
- 🌐 EN / ES
- Saves preference
- Applies to all content

### Core Messages

**1. What It Is**

- ✅ Clarity (understood by 95%+ of test audience)
- ✅ Cultural appropriateness
- ✅ Emotional impact (informative, not traumatizing)
- ✅ Actionability (clear next steps)
- ✅ Accessibility (plain language, 8th grade level)
