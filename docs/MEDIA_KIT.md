### Long (200 words)

Shomer ICE Alerts is an innovative community safety feature that provides verified notifications about reported immigration enforcement activity. Built on principles of accuracy, dignity, and privacy, the system addresses a critical gap in how immigrant communities receive and share safety information.

Every alert undergoes human moderation by trained reviewers before publication, with rigorous checks to ensure no personal information is included. Shomer developed ICE Alerts in partnership with sanctuary congregations, legal aid organizations, and immigrant rights advocates. The service reflects deep community input and follows a comprehensive policy framework that emphasizes transparency, accountability, and community trust.

The platform is part of Shomer's broader mission to provide technology that serves vulnerable communities with respect, accuracy, and unwavering commitment to privacy and human dignity.

## Fact Sheet

### What is Shomer ICE Alerts?

A community-verified notification system that provides timely information about reported immigration enforcement activity.

### How Does It Work?

1. Community members or verified partners submit reports
2. Trained moderators review for accuracy and privacy (within 15 minutes)
3. Approved alerts sent to opt-in subscribers via SMS, email, or push notification
4. Each alert includes "Know Your Rights" resources
5. Alerts automatically expire after 48 hours

### Who Can Receive Alerts?

- Anyone who opts in (explicit consent required)
- Free for all community members
- Available in English and Español
- Geographic customization (choose areas of interest)

### What Information is Shared in Alerts?

**Included:**
- General area/neighborhood
- Type of activity observed
- Severity level (rumor/verified/active)
- Date and time
- Legal resources and hotlines

**NOT Included:**
- Specific addresses
- Names of individuals
- Personal identifying information
- Photos of people

### What Privacy Protections Exist?

- No immigration status collected
- Minimal data retention (48 hours public, 7 days archive)
- No sharing with law enforcement
- Encryption of all data
- Annual transparency reports
- No IP logging for public access

### Who Is Behind Shomer?

Shomer is a community safety platform committed to serving vulnerable populations with respect and dignity. The ICE Alerts feature was developed in close partnership with:
- 3 sanctuary congregations
- 1 legal aid organization
- Immigration rights advocates
- Community members with lived experience

### How Is This Legal?

- Protected by First Amendment (free speech)
- Reports publicly observable activity
- Does not obstruct law enforcement
- Similar to neighborhood watch, traffic alerts
- Reviewed by legal counsel

## Interview Preparation

### Likely Questions & Suggested Responses

**Q: Is this legal?**

A: Yes, absolutely. Providing information about publicly observable government activity is protected by the First Amendment. This is similar to traffic alerts, neighborhood watch programs, or local news. We report only publicly observable activity and do not interfere with law enforcement operations. We've had extensive legal review.

**Q: Doesn't this help people evade law enforcement?**

A: We provide general information about reported activity in neighborhoods, similar to how news stations report on traffic or police activity. We don't provide real-time tactical information, and we don't target specific individuals. Our alerts are retrospective, not predictive. The information we share is publicly observable.

**Q: What if there's a false alert?**

A: Every alert is human-reviewed by trained moderators, which significantly reduces the risk of false information. If an error does occur, we immediately send a correction to all recipients, investigate the cause, and update our processes. We publish transparency reports on our accuracy rates.

**Q: How do you protect user privacy?**

A: Privacy-by-design is core to everything we do. We don't collect immigration status, we minimize data retention (48 hours for active alerts), we don't share data with law enforcement, and we encrypt all information. Users can access the public feed without even creating an account.

**Q: Who funds this?**

A: [Your funding information - grants, donations, etc.]. This is a community service, not a profit-driven venture. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't charge users.

**Q: What makes Shomer different from social media?**

A: Social media spreads unverified rumors that can cause panic. Every Shomer alert is reviewed by a trained human moderator, checked for accuracy and privacy compliance, and includes verified legal resources. We prioritize accuracy over speed and calm over panic.
